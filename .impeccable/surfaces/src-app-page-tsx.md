# Home page (single-page portfolio)

Scope: the whole single page at `/` (header, hero, about, experience, projects, contact, footer). Mode: Persuade. Success is a visitor reaching out through the contact form.

Audience and job: hiring teams and prospective clients deciding whether George is the engineering and product leader they need, then contacting him. Proof on hand: CTO & VP, Product at Cyberscope (verified on cyberscope.io/about-us, no start date published), Software Engineer at Cyberscope Nov 2022 – May 2025 with formal smart contract audits, three live projects. No metrics: outcome slots stay out until George supplies numbers.

Constraints: dark theme is the default for new visitors, light and system stay selectable. Avoid crypto hype (neon, glow, chain motifs) and generic dev-template patterns. No currency marks, coins, or denominations.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio printed like a security document engineered not to be forged, read like a component datasheet. Refuses the centred name hero over a row of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Steel intaglio plate (dark, default) and printed security paper (light). Code-generated guilloché rosettes in fine silver and iris-teal line, microprint rules that carry real text, serial-style mono data, copper as the single action colour. Commissioner with its flare axis for engraved display type; Martian Mono only for data, dates, and microprint. Datasheet tables with hairline rules, no cards.

STORY: The visitor sees who George is and his current title in one glance, reads the datasheet proof (roles, stack, shipped work, location), checks the record and the projects with their architecture, and ends at the contact slip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A large guilloché medallion engraves itself upper right and bleeds off the edge, with a hidden GV latent image that surfaces as the pointer moves (scroll on touch). Top left: the title line, a one-paragraph lede, and a copper Get in touch plus Download CV. A solid data plate of spec rows overlaps the medallion's lower edge. "George Vlassis" runs across the bottom of the viewport at full width. Microprint rules frame the band.

FORM: Security Print (intaglio security printing), position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, fused at roughly 30% with position 1, Component Datasheet, at the user's request. Seed key cc55a420.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: the latent image in the medallion, driven by pointer (desktop) and scroll (touch), static under reduced motion. Motion grammar: one engraving draw-in on load with exponential ease-out, and the theme switch as a single plate-press wipe. Nothing else animates on entry.

Raises kept from declined challengers: honest counts from data (Minihompy), one measured lattice where every rule says something (Oscilloscope), a full spec card beside each project plate (Glaze Shelf), the theme switch as one linked transformation (Miura), rosettes as a numbered unit set captioned with their parameters (Breton), every section points to the contact slip (Quiz).

Unresolved: CTO start date; engineering challenges and outcomes per project need George's input; CV PDF predates the CTO title.
